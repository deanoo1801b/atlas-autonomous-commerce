# ATLAS Approval Policy

## Always approval-required
- Public publishing
- Spending or paid advertising
- Payment/account/security changes
- Placing supplier/customer orders
- Contracts and material external commitments
- Financial trades
- Destructive deletion
- Major strategic changes

## Autonomous preparation allowed
- Research
- Evidence collection
- Analytics
- Trend scanning
- Draft content
- Opportunity reports
- Experiment designs
- SEO research
- Margin analysis
- Product/market eligibility checks

Default runtime mode is DRY_RUN. Protected actions remain blocked until explicit approval.
