# Instagram Growth & Monetisation Intelligence Layer

## Source framework incorporated
The social system should continuously apply five principles from the supplied Instagram framework:

1. Account audit — review profile positioning, bio, content, captions, hooks, visuals, posting strategy and engagement.
2. 30-day content strategy — build a rolling 30-day plan around reach, discovery, engagement and conversion, with repeatable formats and strong hooks.
3. Growth strategy — optimise Reels, carousels, Stories, hooks, CTAs and daily engagement around measurable audience growth. No guaranteed follower targets.
4. Monetisation strategy — connect audience intent to relevant products/offers, pricing, content angles, CTAs and an appropriate sales journey.
5. Weekly social-manager loop — every week analyse performance, identify what worked/failed, improve content, refresh hooks/captions/profile and produce the next action plan.

## ATLAS adaptation

The framework is a strategic input, not a promise of "$100,000/month", "viral" results or guaranteed growth.

### Audit dimensions
- Profile positioning and clarity
- Bio and CTA
- Profile-to-offer alignment
- Reels and opening hooks
- Carousel usefulness
- Story engagement
- Caption quality
- Visual consistency
- Posting cadence
- Saves, shares, comments, reach and watch-time signals where available
- Product-page/landing-page relevance
- Conversion signals where measurable

### Content engine
The Social Media Manager should maintain a rolling 30-day calendar and continuously generate:
- educational/problem-solving content
- product demonstrations
- discovery content
- community questions
- behind-the-scenes content
- product/offer content
- repurposable concepts across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and Pinterest where appropriate

### Growth loop
Audit -> Identify bottleneck -> Hypothesis -> Create variant -> Publish after approval -> Measure -> Learn -> Refresh

Prioritise:
- stronger first 1–3 seconds
- useful/searchable topics
- saves and shares
- comments/conversation
- clear CTAs
- consistent series
- platform-native formatting

Do not optimise for follower count alone.

### Monetisation loop
Audience signal -> Relevant product/offer -> Evidence check -> Content angle -> CTA -> Landing page -> Conversion measurement

Every commercial post must:
- use the correct product URL when applicable
- verify availability where required
- use substantiated claims
- disclose commercial/AI content where required
- avoid fake reviews, fake scarcity and unsupported outcomes

### Weekly manager report
The Social Media Manager should produce:
- Top-performing content
- Weakest-performing content
- Hook patterns
- Topic patterns
- Format/platform patterns
- Engagement signals
- Traffic/conversion signals where attributable
- Products/offers receiving attention
- Content to stop
- Content to improve
- New tests
- Next 7-day action plan

### Decision rules
- A high reach post is not automatically a winner.
- A high engagement post is not automatically a sales winner.
- A sales winner requires measured evidence.
- Competitor performance is a market signal, not proof of Atlas demand.
- Follower growth is a KPI, not a guarantee.
- No autonomous publishing, spending or major commercial changes.

This layer feeds the ATLAS Social Media Manager, Analytics & Attribution, Experiment/A-B Testing, Conversion Optimisation and Opportunity Report systems.
