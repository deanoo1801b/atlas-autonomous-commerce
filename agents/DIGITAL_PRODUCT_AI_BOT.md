# Digital Product AI Bot — Agent Operating System

## Mission
Research, create, optimize, distribute, and sell digital products across the user's brands, with human approval before commercial publishing.

## Core agents
1. Orchestrator / Overlord — coordinates agents, queues work, checks dependencies, and reports status.
2. Global Trends & Demand Intelligence — Google Trends, seasonal demand, search behavior, marketplace signals, YouTube/TikTok signals.
3. Product Opportunity & Market Validation — identifies gaps, validates demand, competition, and buyer intent.
4. Digital Product Writer — creates ebooks, workbooks, checklists, planners, templates, guides, and supporting copy.
5. PDF Design & Production — converts approved concepts into customer-ready PDF products and printable variants.
6. Image Generation — covers, product mockups, social graphics, Pinterest assets, ads, illustrations.
7. Video Creation — short-form videos, product explainers, UGC-style concepts, YouTube Shorts/Reels/TikTok assets.
8. SEO & Global Sales — keyword research, international SEO, localization, product metadata, landing-page optimization, global market expansion.
9. Organic Content & PDF Distribution — creates SEO articles and supporting content, matches products to search intent, distributes organically, repurposes content across channels, and tracks traffic/conversions.
10. Shopify Product & Store Agent — product creation, collections, metadata, digital delivery, merchandising, and store optimization.
11. Social Media Manager — content calendar, platform adaptation, scheduling, monitoring, and performance feedback.
12. Pricing & Profit Agent — pricing, fees, margin, bundles, upsells, and profitability checks.
13. Competitor Intelligence — monitors competitor offers, positioning, content, pricing, and product gaps.
14. Product Repurposing & Bundle Agent — turns one core product into variants, bundles, templates, lead magnets, and platform-specific editions.
15. Marketplace Expansion — evaluates suitable channels such as Shopify, Etsy, Amazon/KDP, and other relevant marketplaces.
16. Customer Demand & Feedback — reviews questions, reviews, comments, and customer behavior for product improvements.
17. Copyright / Compliance Agent — checks originality, licensing, trademarks, claims, and platform requirements.
18. Visual & Product QC — final page-by-page, image, formatting, metadata, and listing checks.
19. Analytics & Experimentation — tracks traffic, conversion, sales, content performance, and controlled tests.
20. Asset / Knowledge Manager — keeps product files, prompts, templates, versions, and research organized.
21. Sales Forecasting — monitors product performance and supports content/prioritization decisions.
22. Automation / Integration Agent — coordinates GitHub, Shopify, Canva, Metricool, image/video tooling, and future integrations.

## Operating flow
Trend research -> opportunity validation -> product brief -> content/product creation -> image/video assets -> QC -> SEO/listing -> organic distribution -> human approval -> publishing -> analytics -> optimization -> repurposing.

## Publishing gate
No agent should autonomously publish a new commercial product, paid advertisement, or irreversible store change without human approval unless a future workflow explicitly authorizes it.

## First implementation milestone
Build the shared agent specification, project configuration, task states, approval gates, and integration map in GitHub before adding automated publishing.
