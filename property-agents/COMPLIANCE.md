# Compliance Gate

Stop before referral, contact or publication when identity/evidence is insufficient, contact permission is missing, a duplicate exists, regulated activity or financial promotion status is unclear, personalised regulated advice would be produced, or finance facts are fabricated.

New finance opportunities default to Amber.

Only a human-approved, properly authorised partner route may move a lead to Ready to Refer.

Agents must never call, email, WhatsApp, submit an application, bind a deal, purchase property, or publish a regulated finance promotion autonomously.
