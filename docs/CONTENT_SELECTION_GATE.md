# Atlas Goods Content Selection Gate

No product video or product-led social post is created until this gate passes.

## Pipeline

Shopify Catalogue
→ Product Opportunity & Evidence Agent
→ Evidence Report
→ ATLAS Decision Gate
→ Creative/Video Agent
→ Compliance Check
→ Metricool Scheduler
→ Publish
→ Social + Shopify Performance
→ Learning loop

## Mandatory pre-video report

Before every product video, ATLAS must receive:

1. Product name
2. Product URL
3. Measurement period
4. Shopify traffic/hits and visitors where available
5. Orders/revenue/conversion signals where available
6. Current market/trend signals
7. Previous Atlas content performance for the product/category where available
8. Stock/availability
9. Why the product is being tested
10. Proposed content angle
11. Supported product facts
12. Claims that must not be made
13. Evidence limitations

## Hard stop conditions

Do not create or schedule the video if:
- required product evidence is missing;
- the product URL is unavailable;
- the product is unavailable when availability is required;
- the proposed creative depends on an unsupported claim;
- data is being represented as more certain than it is;
- the content would copy another advertiser's creative or protected material;
- the required platform/media connection is unavailable.

## Transparency rule

The report must say exactly what the evidence means.

Example:

"127 product-page sessions in the last 30 days" is acceptable.

"Best-selling product" is not acceptable unless supported by appropriate sales data.

"203 active competitor ads" is an advertising/trend signal, not proof that Atlas Goods sold 203 units.

## Approval rule

The evidence report is produced before creative generation. The video agent cannot bypass the gate.
