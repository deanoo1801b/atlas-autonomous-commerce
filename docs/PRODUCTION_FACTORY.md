# ATLAS Digital Product Factory

The factory converts a validated product opportunity into a controlled production package.

## Flow

Validated opportunity
→ Product brief
→ Content structure
→ PDF/product assets
→ Cover and mockups
→ Listing copy
→ Visual/content QC
→ Human approval
→ Publishing

## Required brief

- Product title
- Target audience
- Customer problem
- Product promise
- Product format
- Sections/content structure
- Keywords
- Market evidence
- Commercial assumptions

## Asset plan

Every product starts with a cover, customer-facing interior file, product mockup, organic social asset and listing copy.

## Safety

Production can create drafts and assets, but the package remains publishing-blocked until QC and explicit approval are completed.
