# ATLAS Intelligence Pipeline

Global Trends & Demand Intelligence
↓
Digital Product Opportunity Hunter
↓
Market Validation
↓
Profit & Pricing Intelligence
↓
Digital Product Factory

## Rules

- Trend signals are evidence, not automatic product decisions.
- Opportunities must identify a specific audience, problem and product format.
- Validation must separate observed evidence from assumptions.
- Pricing must model platform fees and target margin.
- No commercial publishing occurs in this layer.
- Weak evidence sends an opportunity back for research rather than forcing production.

## Initial product output

An opportunity becomes a candidate for production only when it has:
1. Clear demand evidence.
2. Defined audience/problem.
3. Specific digital product format.
4. Competitive context.
5. Commercial viability assessment.
6. Confidence score.
