# ATLAS Social Media Agent Workflow

## Purpose
Run the social-media specialist layer as a controlled pipeline.

## Agent order

1. Trend Research — identify current formats, topics and product/content signals.
2. Customer Demand — identify real audience questions and problems.
3. Competitor Intelligence — identify market patterns without copying protected creative.
4. SEO Opportunity — identify search-led content opportunities.
5. Product Opportunity — verify product availability, URL and evidence.
6. Analytics & Attribution — provide historical Atlas performance context.
7. Conversion Optimisation — check destination/product-page readiness.
8. Social Media Manager — create the platform-specific content plan.
9. Creative & Video Production — prepare original assets.
10. Compliance & Brand QA — check claims, disclosures, URLs and brand rules.
11. Experiment & A/B Testing — define controlled tests.
12. Approval Gate — protected publishing remains blocked until explicit approval.
13. Metricool — scheduling/publishing only after approval.
14. Analytics & Attribution — measure results.
15. Experiment & A/B Testing — feed measured results into the next cycle.

## Platform scope

The manager can prepare content for:
- TikTok
- Instagram
- Facebook
- YouTube Shorts
- Pinterest

Actual availability must be verified from the connected publishing platform before scheduling.

## Required content record

Every proposed post should contain:
- platform
- product/content subject
- exact product URL when applicable
- hook
- script/caption
- CTA
- media requirement
- disclosure requirement
- evidence sources
- approval status
- experiment ID
- timestamp

## Hard stops

Do not publish when:
- the product is unavailable where availability is required
- the URL is missing or invalid
- claims lack evidence
- required media is missing
- the platform connection is unavailable
- compliance QA fails
- explicit approval is required but absent

The social layer prepares and learns autonomously; protected public publishing remains controlled.
