# Opportunity Report Runtime

The Opportunity Report is the central evidence layer between research and action.

## Required gates
1. Evidence exists and is timestamped.
2. Market eligibility is verified.
3. Availability is verified when required.
4. Product URL is present when applicable.
5. Confidence and uncertainty are recorded.
6. Social/organic opportunities remain recommendations until approved.
7. Public publishing, spending and external commitments remain approval-gated.

## Market routing
- Digital products: generally worldwide, subject to platform/payment eligibility.
- GoDropship physical products: UK only unless a verified rule changes.
- Gelato POD: country targeting must be verified for the specific product/SKU and fulfilment route.

## Status lifecycle
NEW -> TEST -> HOLD / BLOCKED / ARCHIVED
TEST -> HOLD / ARCHIVED
No status implies a guaranteed sale or bestseller.
