# ATLAS Email Funnel Engine

ATLAS should build an owned-audience layer around every validated digital-product opportunity.

## Best ideas taken from the observed model

- One-time funnel setup that can be reused and improved.
- Automated lead capture and nurture.
- Lead magnet → welcome → nurture → offer → post-purchase → win-back.
- Measure the entire funnel, not just product sales.
- Optimise revenue per subscriber and lead-to-sale conversion.
- Reuse proven funnels across related products and bundles.

## ATLAS implementation

The Email Funnel & Lifecycle Agent creates a funnel plan after market validation and pricing. The coordinator can route an opportunity to this agent when the opportunity contains email, funnel, lead-magnet, subscriber or lifecycle signals.

## Important distinction

The system may learn from commercial examples such as MailerLite's positioning, but ATLAS does not assume MailerLite is connected. The email-marketing integration remains unconfigured until a provider is explicitly connected and verified.

## Safety

Email copy, automations and promotional sends remain approval-gated. ATLAS must respect consent, unsubscribe requirements, applicable privacy law and platform policies.

## Measurement loop

Traffic → lead capture → email engagement → offer conversion → revenue per subscriber → retention/win-back.

A funnel is scaled only when measured results meet predefined criteria.
